AirTennisWatch

挥拍即响。手表识别你的网球动作，击球时播放声音，并记下这一拍的球速和力度。 Sound on contact. The watch recognizes your tennis swing, plays the hit, and records that shot’s speed and power.

功能FEATURES

五种动作Five strokes

正手、反手、发球、正手削球、反手削球。Forehand, backhand, serve, forehand slice, and backhand slice.

击球就有声音Sound on contact

屏幕同时给出动作名称、估算球速（km/h），以及轻挡、抽球或扣杀。The screen shows the stroke, an estimated ball speed in km/h, and the power: block, drive, or smash.

训练留在腕上Stats on the wrist

今日挥拍、最高球速、每个动作的拍数和球速，还有近 7 天的挥拍量。Today’s swings, top speed, the count and speed of each stroke, and the last 7 days.

按你的动作校准Calibrate your swing

某一类经常认错时，只为这个动作录几拍，生成个人模型。也可以恢复默认。When one stroke keeps getting misread, record a few reps of just that stroke and build a personal model. You can return to the default.

健康可以以后再开Health can wait

锻炼中显示心率和活动消耗，结束时写入「健康」。不授权也能挥拍和看统计。Heart rate and active calories show during a workout and can be saved to Health. Swings and stats work without permission.

用法HOW TO USE

  1. 01在已配对的 iPhone 上安装，再到 Apple Watch 上打开空气网球。Install it on the paired iPhone, then open Air Tennis on Apple Watch.
  2. 02按平时的方式握拍、挥拍。主页上写着「挥拍即响」。Swing the way you usually do. The home screen reads “挥拍即响”.
  3. 03看记录点「统计」。想让某一拍更准，点「校准」。Tap 统计 (Stats) for your record. Tap 校准 (Calibrate) when a stroke should fit you better.
  4. 04要测心率时，点「开始网球锻炼（测心率）」。打完点「结束并写入健康」。For heart rate, tap 开始网球锻炼（测心率）. When you finish, tap 结束并写入「健康」.

权限ACCESS

运动传感器Motion

用来判断这次是不是挥拍，以及是哪一种动作。识别需要这项权限。Used to tell a swing from other movement, and which stroke it is. Recognition needs this.

健康，可选Health, optional

你开始锻炼并同意之后，才读取心率、活动消耗，并把锻炼写入健身 App。之后也可以在 iPhone「健康 → 共享 → App」里打开。Heart rate and active calories are read only after you start a workout and allow it. You can turn this on later in the iPhone Health app, under Sharing → Apps.
